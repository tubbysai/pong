How To Play
🖱️
Move: Move your mouse or finger over the table — your paddle follows your cursor in the lower foreground.
🏓
Hit: Move your paddle into the ball's path. Contact happens automatically when the ball is in reach.
⬆️
Topspin: Flick upward right as you hit the ball to add topspin — it dives down fast after crossing the net.
⬇️
Backspin: Flick downward on contact for backspin — the ball floats and skids low off the bounce.
↔️
Sidespin: Flick sideways to curve the ball left or right in flight.
🎯
Aim: Where your paddle is moving when it strikes the ball determines the shot direction and power — a fast, deliberate swing hits harder.
🤾
Serve: Press and hold to toss the ball up, then swipe to strike it. It must bounce on your side first, then land on the opponent's side.
🔁
Scoring: Play to 11 points, win by 2. Serve alternates every 2 points (every point during deuce).
